# Contract 2.0 architecture

## Boundary and taxonomy

Custom GPT researches and supplies narrative reasoning. The runtime generates no market research, moat, management, forecast rationale, counter-thesis or AI-ranking rationale. It validates contracts, time, ownership, calculation inputs, identities, progression and durable state. `FACTS`, `COMPANY_CLAIMS`, `EXTERNAL_ESTIMATES`, `AI_ASSUMPTIONS`, `JUDGMENTS`, and `UNRESOLVED` are separate records. Every judgment cites supporting and contrary evidence, dependency roots, confidence, uncertainty and invalidation.

## Blind protocol and funnel

Pipeline intake exposes identity, theme membership, raw evidence, quality, cutoff and horizons only. Candidate display is normalized-ID order and fixed per generation. Upstream scores and recommendations remain sealed until Initial Phase 10's independent AI ordinal ranking is persisted and hashed. Phase 11 cannot mutate that ranking. Explicit set identities track initial (≤12), eligible (≤8), deep (≤5), and final (≤2 or empty); excluded candidates remain in history. Exploratory proposals never mutate the current set.

## Initial 12 / Update 4

Initial phases are: (1) identity/horizon/quality, (2) eligibility/metric vocabulary/comparability, (3) causal theme exposure, (4) business/moat/technology, (5) management/allocation/governance, (6) financial quality and deep set, (7) catalysts/risks/cruxes/dependency roots, (8) valuation and frozen assumptions, (9) common exclusive scenarios and overlays, (10) blind independent AI ranking freeze, (11) mechanical disclosure/adversarial pairs/sensitivity, and (12) integrated selection, dual handoffs and ledger.

Updates are: (1) strictly newer generation/candidate/evidence diff, (2) blind independent reassessment, (3) deterministic recalculation/reconciliation/counter-thesis, and (4) integrated reselection plus atomic handoff supersession. Exact `更新` starts it and exact `次` advances one phase.

## Four non-overwriting results

1. Evidence-only mechanical ranking accepts only facts and eligible external estimates; unavailable is not zero.
2. Scenario-derived ranking declares AI assumptions and uses deterministic arithmetic.
3. Independent AI ranking is ordinal and frozen while both machine rankings are hidden.
4. Integrated selection follows integrity, eligibility, quality, hard-risk, benchmark, three results, adversarial evidence, robustness, horizons and user policy in sequence—not an average.

Hard gates cannot be offset or AI-overridden. Negative/benchmark-inferior returns, permanent-loss failures, fragile ranks and horizon conflict constrain classification. No qualifying candidate produces formal `NO_SELECTION`.

All unordered deep-set pairs require arguments in both directions and reversal conditions; Condorcet cycles are retained. Sensitivity covers growth, margin, multiple, dilution, capex, discount rate, probabilities, terminal growth, adoption timing and execution probability.

## Handoffs, ledger and outcomes

The first individual-analysis handoff contains only identity, cutoff facts, quality, horizons, diluted-share definition and checks. Runtime acknowledgement is required before reconciliation ranks, reasons, objections, assumptions and ledger identity are released. Each candidate/horizon ledger stores all four ranks, gates, selection, overrides, returns, risk, robustness, catalysts, invalidation and artifact hashes. Outcome records support 3/6/12/24/36 months; data before maturity is `not_matured`, and evidence after the decision cutoff is rejected as decision input.
